# Product Vision

SpeakCareer helps professionals practice English conversations that matter at work.

The app should feel like a calm practice partner for job interviews, meetings, presentations, sales calls and workplace conversations. The user should be able to practice a realistic scenario, receive clear feedback and know exactly what to improve next.

## Positioning

"Practice job interviews, meetings and professional conversations with AI."

## First Audience

Non-native English speakers who want better professional communication for interviews, meetings and career growth.

## First Language

English.

## Later Languages

Spanish, French and Mandarin Chinese are planned after the English MVP proves useful.

## Product Principles

- Professional before playful.
- Practical feedback before broad content.
- Small daily progress before long lessons.
- Simple UX before complex personalization.
- Backend-only AI calls when OpenAI is added later.
