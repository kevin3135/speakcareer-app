# MVP Definition

The MVP is a focused English practice app for professional conversations.

## Must Have

- Onboarding with clear positioning.
- Home screen with a recommended next practice.
- Practice screen with short modules and roleplay library.
- Roleplay screen with realistic workplace scenarios.
- Mock AI feedback UI with scores, strengths, improvements and rewrite.
- Progress screen with summary and mistake bank.
- Profile screen with language plan and future integration status.
- Local mock content only.

## Not in MVP

- Real OpenAI calls.
- Supabase auth or database.
- Payments or RevenueCat.
- Analytics or PostHog.
- Error tracking or Sentry.
- App Store or Google Play setup.
- Full Spanish, French or Mandarin Chinese content.

## Success Criteria

- A founder can open the app and understand the product direction in under one minute.
- A user can choose one roleplay and see useful mock feedback.
- The app structure is simple enough for future Codex runs to extend safely.
